Our Story

Omar Phone was founded in 2026 by Omar Al-Mehrat, a passionate technology enthusiast who recognized a gap in the mobile phone retail market. Starting as a small kiosk in a bustling Eygpt shopping district, Omar's vision was simple: provide customers with premium smartphones at fair prices while delivering exceptional service. What began with just a handful of phones and a dream has grown into one of the region's most trusted mobile phone retailers.

Over the years, we have expanded our operations from that single kiosk to a fully-fledged retail presence with both physical stores and a comprehensive online platform. Our growth has been driven by an unwavering commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction. Today, we proudly serve thousands of customers across the UAE, offering everything from the latest flagship devices to certified pre-owned smartphones, all backed by our industry-leading warranty and support.

Mission & Vision

Our Vision
