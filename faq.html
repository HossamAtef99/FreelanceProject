Orders

Simply browse our shop, select the device you want, choose your preferred storage and color options, and click "Add to Cart." Once you are ready, proceed to checkout where you will enter your shipping details and payment information. You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after your order is placed successfully.

We accept all major credit and debit cards including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. We also support PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfers, and cash on delivery for selected locations within the UAE. All payments are processed through secure, encrypted gateways to ensure your financial information remains safe.

Shipping

Delivery times vary depending on your location. For deliveries within Dubai and the UAE, orders are typically delivered within 1–3 business days. For international orders, delivery usually takes 5–10 business days depending on the destination and customs clearance procedures.

We offer free shipping on all orders within the UAE. For international orders, shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the destination, package weight, and selected shipping method. Express shipping options are available at an additional cost for customers who need their devices urgently.

Returns

We offer a 14-day return policy on all devices from the date of delivery. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return it in its original condition with all accessories and packaging for a full refund or exchange. Please contact our support team to initiate the return process.

To qualify for a return, the device must be in the same condition as received, with no physical damage, scratches, or signs of wear. It must include all original accessories, packaging, manuals, and any promotional items. Devices with user-inflicted damage, water damage, or unauthorized modifications will not be accepted for return.

Once we receive and inspect your returned device, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The amount will be credited back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take an additional 3–7 business days for the refund to appear in your account.

Warranty

Our 12-month warranty covers all manufacturing defects including hardware malfunctions, battery defects, screen issues, and charging port problems that arise during normal use. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized repairs, or normal wear and tear such as cosmetic scratches.

To claim your warranty, simply contact our support team via phone, email, or WhatsApp with your order number and a description of the issue. Our team will guide you through the process, which may include sending the device to our service center for inspection. Once the issue is confirmed as a manufacturing defect, we will repair or replace the device at no cost.
